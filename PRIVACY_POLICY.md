# Privacy Policy

**Project:** Kyron™ V2  
**Maintained by:** uwerva  
**Last Updated:** November 8, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes the general privacy principles applicable to Kyron™ V2 and any related website, dashboard, or services made available for the project (collectively, the "Services").

The information processed by a particular deployment depends on the features enabled, its configuration, and the hosting environment. This policy does not claim that every possible feature or data-processing activity is enabled in every deployment.

## 2. Information and Content

When you use a deployment of the Services, technical information may be processed as part of normal operation. Depending on the enabled features and configuration, this may include:

- Information you choose to provide when requesting support.
- Prompts, messages, files, or other content submitted to an enabled feature.
- Technical events, timestamps, and diagnostic information generated during operation.
- Basic device or connection information made available through ordinary web or server requests.

The exact information handled depends on the deployment. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless the relevant feature is intended to receive it and you are authorized to provide it.

## 3. How Information May Be Used

Where applicable to the deployment, information may be used to:

- Operate the Services and provide requested functionality.
- Respond to support requests.
- Diagnose errors and maintain reliability.
- Protect the Services against misuse and security incidents.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Storage and Retention

Storage locations and retention periods depend on the hosting environment and configuration used by a particular deployment. Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, or as required by applicable law.

No universal retention-period promise is made for independently hosted or modified deployments.

## 5. Third-Party Services

A deployment may rely on third-party hosting providers, APIs, libraries, or integrations. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

The use of a third-party service depends on the configuration of the deployment. Users should review the applicable provider policies when using an integration.

## 6. Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect information handled by the Services. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users and operators should protect credentials, restrict access to private data, and avoid publishing API keys, passwords, or tokens.

## 7. Privacy Choices and Requests

Privacy rights vary depending on the laws that apply to you and the way the relevant deployment is operated.

For questions about the public project, use the repository's available contact or support channels. For security-sensitive reports, follow the reporting guidance published in the repository. Do not post private personal information or credentials in public issue reports.

If a deployment is operated by another person or organization, that operator is responsible for explaining its own data practices and handling requests relating to that deployment.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Operators must use the Services in compliance with applicable laws concerning children's privacy and personal information. The Services should not be used to knowingly collect children's personal information unlawfully.

## 9. International Processing

Where a deployment uses infrastructure located in another country, information may be processed in that country, subject to the laws applicable to the operator and the deployment.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the project or its privacy practices change. The latest version will be published in the project repository with its updated date.
