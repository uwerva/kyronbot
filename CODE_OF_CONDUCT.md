# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who participates in this project.

We welcome contributions from people of all experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected Behavior

All contributors and community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate clearly and constructively.
- Welcome feedback and different perspectives.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Give credit where it is due.
- Respect the project's license and attribution requirements.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Threatening or intimidating language.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it through the project's available support or repository channels.

Please include relevant context and avoid sharing private information publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may review reported incidents and take appropriate action. Actions may include a warning, removal of content, or restriction from project spaces, depending on the circumstances.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other community spaces when someone is representing the project.
