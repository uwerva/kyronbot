06. WHAT A DMCA REQUEST IS

06.01 The United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act is commonly abbreviated as the DMCA.
06.02 Section 512 of Title 17 of the United States Code establishes certain notice-and-takedown procedures.
06.03 A DMCA notice is a formal allegation that identified online material infringes a copyright.
06.04 A valid notice must satisfy the requirements applicable to the specific service provider and circumstances.
06.05 The DMCA is not a general-purpose process for every disagreement about online content.
06.06 Trademark disputes, privacy complaints, security reports, and contractual disputes may require different procedures.
06.07 A claimant should identify the copyrighted work and the specific material alleged to be infringing.
06.08 A notice should explain the basis for the claimant's authority to act for the rights holder.
06.09 A takedown request is an allegation and is not, by itself, a court judgment.
06.10 Only the appropriate platform or competent authority can take the actions within its control.
